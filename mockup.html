Living here

Why Coral Gables

The City Beautiful was designed, not just developed.

  • Architecture that holds valueDesign guidelines have protected the Mediterranean character, and property values, for nearly a century.
  • A downtown you can walkMiracle Mile, the Biltmore and daily errands within a few blocks.
  • Strong school zonesAmong the most sought-after in Miami-Dade, a deciding factor for relocating families.
  • A mature tree canopyBanyan- and oak-lined streets that newer developments cannot replicate.
  • Central, without the densityMinutes from Coconut Grove homes for sale, Brickell condos for sale and Miami International Airport.

Questions

Coral Gables Real Estate FAQ

Can I buy in Coral Gables if I live outside the U.S.?

Most foreign buyers can purchase property in Florida without U.S. citizenship or residency. Florida law restricts purchases by buyers from certain countries, so we confirm eligibility on the first call. We coordinate remote tours, international wire and closing logistics, and introduce you to lenders and tax advisors who work with foreign buyers.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Every step, from the first call to the closing documents review, can be handled in Spanish or English.

Can I tour homes without traveling?

We run live video tours and send our own notes on each home and its street, so you only travel for the homes worth seeing.

Which areas of Coral Gables do you cover?

All of them, from Gables Estates and Cocoplum to the Biltmore section, South Gables and the condos around Miracle Mile and Merrick Park.
